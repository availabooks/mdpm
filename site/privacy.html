Privacy policy

mdpm does not collect any data.

The mdpm VS Code extension and CLI read and write files in your own workspace. They send no telemetry, analytics or usage data to us or anyone else, and this website sets no cookies.

When you run an agent such as Claude Code, Codex or Cursor, that tool runs on your machine under its own terms and privacy policy. mdpm only starts it.

Questions? Email availabooks@gmail.com.
